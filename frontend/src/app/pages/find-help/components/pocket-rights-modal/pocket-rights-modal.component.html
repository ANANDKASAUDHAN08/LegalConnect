Know Your Fundamental Arrest & Legal Aid Rights

District: {{ locationQuery }}
1
Right to Know Grounds of Arrest & Bailability

Under Article 22(1) and BNSS Section 47 / CrPC Section 50, police must immediately inform you of the exact offence and whether you are entitled to bail as a matter of right.

2
24-Hour Production Before Magistrate (Art 22(2))

No arrested person can be detained in police custody beyond 24 hours without the express judicial authorization of a Judicial Magistrate.

3
D.K. Basu Guidelines on Arrest Memo & Family Intimation

Police must prepare an Arrest Memo signed by at least one independent witness. You have the right to have a friend or family member notified within 8 to 12 hours of detention.

4
Mandatory Medical Checkup & Special Women Rules

Right to independent medical checkup every 48 hours. No woman can be arrested after sunset and before sunrise except in extraordinary circumstances by a woman police officer.

5
Free Legal Aid Representation (Section 12 NALSA)

Women, children, SC/ST citizens, custody undertrials, and low-income citizens are entitled to 100% free legal defense at State expense via the District Legal Services Authority (DLSA).
